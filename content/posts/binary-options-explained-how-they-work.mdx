> **Risk disclaimer:** Binary options are high-risk instruments. In many countries they are restricted or banned for retail investors, and a large majority of traders lose money. This article is educational only and is not financial advice.

Binary options are often marketed as the "simplest" way to trade — but simple does not mean safe. Here's an honest explanation of how they work and why they carry so much risk.

## What is a binary option?

A binary option is a contract with only two possible outcomes. You predict whether the price of an asset will be **above or below a set level** at a specific time.

- If you're right, you receive a **fixed payout**
- If you're wrong, you lose your **entire stake**

That "all or nothing" structure is where the name *binary* comes from.

## A quick example

Suppose a binary option asks: *Will EUR/USD be above 1.0850 in 5 minutes?*

- You stake $100 on "yes"
- Payout offered: 80%
- If correct → you get $180 (your $100 + $80 profit)
- If wrong → you lose the full $100

## The math that works against you

Here's the part that matters most. Because a loss costs you 100% but a win pays less than 100%, you need to win **more than half the time just to break even**.

| Payout | Break-even win rate |
| --- | --- |
| 90% | ~53% |
| 80% | ~56% |
| 70% | ~59% |

> If a contract pays 80%, you must win roughly **56%** of your trades just to stay flat — before emotions and mistakes are even factored in.

## Why regulators warn about them

- **Negative expected value:** the payout structure favors the broker
- **Short time frames** encourage overtrading and gambling behavior
- **Unregulated platforms** have been linked to fraud in some regions
- **Bans and restrictions** exist in the EU, UK and other jurisdictions for retail clients

## If you still choose to learn

- Start on a **demo account** with no real money
- Never risk more than a tiny, fixed percentage per trade
- Track your win rate against the break-even threshold above
- Treat it as education, not an income plan

## The bottom line

Binary options are easy to understand but hard to profit from — the built-in math tilts against the trader. Know exactly what you're risking, understand the break-even reality, and never trade money you can't afford to lose.
