Most beginners lose money for a small, predictable set of reasons — the same mistakes repeated across crypto, forex and binary trading. The good news is that predictable problems have fixable solutions.

## 1. Trading without a plan

Entering on impulse, with no defined entry, exit or size. **Fix:** write your rules down before the session and trade only setups that match them.

## 2. Risking too much per trade

Betting a large chunk of the account on single trades, so a short losing streak is fatal. **Fix:** cap risk at 1–2% per trade, every time.

## 3. No stop-loss

Hoping a losing position "comes back" instead of accepting a small, planned loss. **Fix:** decide your exit before you enter, and place the stop immediately.

## 4. Overtrading

Taking trades out of boredom, excitement or the urge to "do something." **Fix:** set a maximum number of trades per day and respect it.

## 5. Revenge trading

Trying to win back a loss immediately, usually with a bigger size. **Fix:** a mandatory break after consecutive losses; close the platform if you hit your daily limit.

## 6. Chasing the market (FOMO)

Buying after a move has already happened, at the worst possible price. **Fix:** if you missed the entry, let it go — there is always another setup.

## 7. Misusing leverage

Treating high available leverage as a target rather than a limit. **Fix:** size from your risk, not from what the broker permits.

> Most account blow-ups aren't caused by bad analysis. They're caused by good analysis and terrible position sizing.

## 8. Ignoring costs

Forgetting that spreads, fees and — for binary options — unfavourable payouts eat into every trade. **Fix:** factor costs into your break-even maths before trading.

## 9. No record-keeping

Repeating the same mistakes because they're never reviewed. **Fix:** keep a journal of every trade including your emotional state, and review it weekly.

## 10. Quitting the demo too early

Risking real money before a strategy has proven itself. **Fix:** trade a demo account until you have a documented, positive track record over many trades.

## The pattern behind them all

Notice that most of these are **not** about predicting the market. They're about **discipline, risk and process**. That's the real lesson: the market rewards consistency far more than cleverness.

## A quick self-audit

- Do I have written rules? 
- Is my risk per trade fixed and small? 
- Do I always use a stop-loss? 
- Do I keep a journal?

If you can honestly answer yes to all four, you're already ahead of most beginners.

## The bottom line

You don't need a secret strategy to stop losing — you need to stop making the ten mistakes above. Fix the process, and the results follow.
