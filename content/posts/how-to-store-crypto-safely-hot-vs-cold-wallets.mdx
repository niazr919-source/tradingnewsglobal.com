In crypto there is a famous saying: **"not your keys, not your coins."** How you store your assets matters just as much as which assets you buy. This guide breaks down the main wallet types and the habits that keep your funds safe.

## The two big categories

### Hot wallets (connected to the internet)

Hot wallets are apps or browser extensions that stay online. They are convenient for everyday use and trading.

- **Pros:** fast, free, easy to use
- **Cons:** more exposed to hacks, malware and phishing

### Cold wallets (offline storage)

Cold wallets keep your private keys completely offline — usually on a dedicated hardware device.

- **Pros:** far more secure, ideal for long-term holdings
- **Cons:** cost money, slightly less convenient

## Custodial vs non-custodial

Another important distinction:

| Type | Who holds your keys | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Custodial | A company (exchange) | Leaving coins on an exchange |
| Non-custodial | You | A hardware or self-managed wallet |

With custodial storage, you trust a third party. With non-custodial storage, **you** are fully responsible — there is no "forgot password" button.

## A simple strategy for most people

1. Keep only what you actively trade in a hot wallet or exchange.
2. Move long-term holdings to a cold wallet.
3. Never share your seed phrase — write it down and store it offline.

> Your seed phrase is the master key. Anyone who has it controls your funds. No legitimate service will ever ask for it.

## Security habits that prevent disaster

- Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) using an app, not SMS
- Double-check wallet addresses before sending
- Beware of fake "support" accounts and giveaway scams
- Keep your recovery phrase off any internet-connected device

## The bottom line

There is no single "best" wallet — there is the right wallet for the job. Use hot wallets for convenience and cold wallets for security, never share your seed phrase, and treat every unexpected message as a potential scam. Good storage habits are the cheapest insurance in crypto.
