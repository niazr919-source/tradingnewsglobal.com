The world's most-traded currency pair, **EUR/USD**, is trading in a tight range as markets await the Federal Reserve's next interest-rate decision. With inflation cooling unevenly and labor data mixed, the rate path — not just the next move — will decide direction.

## The setup into the FOMC

Rate differentials remain the dominant driver for the euro-dollar. A more dovish Fed narrows the yield advantage of the dollar, typically lifting EUR/USD; a hawkish hold does the opposite.

### Three scenarios

1. **Dovish cut / dovish hold** — EUR/USD breaks resistance and extends higher on a weaker dollar.
2. **Hawkish hold** — the dollar firms, pressuring the pair back toward range support.
3. **Data-dependent ambiguity** — choppy, headline-driven price action with elevated intraday volatility.

## Key technical levels

| Level | Type | Significance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Range highs | Resistance | A daily close above opens continuation |
| Mid-range pivot | Balance | Where price mean-reverts intraday |
| Range lows | Support | A break signals dollar strength |

> Around central-bank events, spreads widen and slippage rises — size positions for the volatility, not the average day.

## Positioning around the decision

- Avoid oversized exposure into the announcement itself
- Wait for the initial spike to resolve before committing to a direction
- Use the statement **and** the press conference as separate catalysts

## Bottom line

EUR/USD is a rate-differential story first and a technical story second. Let the Fed set the tone, then trade the reaction with disciplined risk controls.
